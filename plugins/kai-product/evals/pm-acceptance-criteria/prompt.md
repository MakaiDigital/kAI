Here's ticket PAY-202. Can you turn it into acceptance criteria engineering can build against?

Problem: shoppers can heart a product to save it but can't remove it again; support tickets show shoppers asking how to clear old favorites after plans change.
Outcome: a signed-in shopper can remove a product from favorites and it no longer shows in their list, on web and app.
Success metric: support tickets about stuck favorites drop to zero within a month.
Constraints: signed-out shoppers can't change favorites.
Out of scope: bulk removal, sharing favorites.
